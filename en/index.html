Direct EU funds

Why direct EU funds

They are not the usual regional grants. Here is what changes for your company.

1

Managed by the EU

Run directly by the European Commission and its agencies, not by Italian regions or ministries.

2

A European competition

Applications are submitted at EU level: fewer Italian companies compete for them.

3

Grants often higher

For innovation, environment, digital and culture projects, even for a single SME.

It's for you if you are…

  • Owners of Italian companies living abroad: we work with you in English and deal with the Italian side.
  • SMEs with a growth project: a new product, process or market.
  • Innovative startups with technology ready to scale.
  • Companies investing in energy efficiency, circular economy and digital.

FAQ

Before you write to us

All questions
I don't speak Italian. Is that a problem?+

No. We work with you in English and handle the Italian paperwork and communication with Italian bodies on your behalf. EU applications themselves are usually written in English.

Can my company apply?+

Companies registered in Italy can access many direct calls. Eligibility and partnership requirements vary by programme: we check them for free.

Does the grant cover all costs?+

Often not: many calls require co-financing from your company. We tell you clearly before we start.
