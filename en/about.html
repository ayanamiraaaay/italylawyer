About us

Legal experts and consultants specialised in EU direct-management funds. Independent, and on your company's side.

Specialists in EU direct-management funding

We are legal experts and consultants who work with European Commission calls every day. We know the programme rules, the criteria evaluators use to score proposals, and the obligations a company takes on after approval.

That is why we can tell you honestly whether a call is right for you, write a solid application and stay with you until the final report.

  • Legal expertise: we go through eligibility rules, the grant agreement and State aid rules with you.
  • Programme knowledge: Horizon Europe, EIC Accelerator, LIFE, Digital Europe, Creative Europe.
  • Applications in English written to the standard European evaluators expect.
  • Independent: no ties to institutions or intermediaries. We work only for you.

Who we work with

Who we help

  • Owners of Italian companies living abroad: we work with you in English and deal with the Italian side.
  • SMEs with a growth project: a new product, process or market.
  • Innovative startups with technology ready to scale.
  • Companies investing in energy efficiency, circular economy and digital.

We only work with companies registered in Italy that have a concrete project. If that's not your case, we'll tell you straight away.
