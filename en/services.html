Services and pricing

You can hand us the whole process or just the part you need. The quote is always in writing and comes before any commitment.

Services

What we do for your company

01Free

Eligibility check and call selection

We find out whether your company and project qualify, before you invest time and money.

  • Company eligibility check
  • Open, compatible calls
  • Estimate of grant and co-financing
  • Deadlines and realistic timing
02

Application preparation

We write the project around the evaluation criteria, so evaluators find what they look for straight away.

  • Measurable objectives and activities
  • Budget built to the call's rules
  • Consortium partners, if required
  • Documents, review and submission
03

Grant management

After approval we stay with you to the final payment, because a grant has to be kept as well as won.

  • Review of the grant agreement
  • Deadlines and beneficiary obligations
  • Expense reporting
  • Support during checks and audits
